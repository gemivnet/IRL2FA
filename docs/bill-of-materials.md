## PCB and assembly

- 2-layer, 77 × 31 mm, 0.8 mm thick, any colour. JLCPCB's minimum order is 5 boards, typically about $2-5 for the bare boards plus shipping.
- Choose the **ENIG** surface finish: a bare pad under the coin cell is its negative contact, and gold makes a lasting contact where HASL tin can oxidise.
- Assembly uses both sides: the joystick is on the front, everything else on the back. Only JLCPCB's Standard PCBA does both sides, and Standard takes nothing smaller than 70 × 70 mm, so order a 1 × 2 panel with edge rails (Panel by JLCPCB). Upload `hardware/fab/irl2fa-gerbers.zip`, `irl2fa-bom-jlcpcb.csv` and `irl2fa-cpl-jlcpcb.csv`; the placement file is already corrected for JLCPCB's footprints and back-side angle convention. The order steps and what to check on the placement preview are in [assembly-review.md](assembly-review.md#ordering-at-jlcpcb).
- The STM32U083 is often low in stock (check the JLC stock column above). JLCPCB's global sourcing, or parts pre-ordered into your JLCPCB parts inventory, work; the STM32U073KCU6 is pin-compatible (no AES, which the firmware doesn't need).
- Extended parts carry a one-off JLCPCB feeder fee each (about $3 per unique part); this BOM has 11 of them.

See [hardware-options.md](hardware-options.md) for why each part was chosen and the alternatives.
